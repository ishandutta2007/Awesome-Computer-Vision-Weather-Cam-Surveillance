## 📊 Sector Overview & Market Dynamics

The global video surveillance and computer vision market is estimated at approximately **$63B–$84B in 2025** and is projected to expand to **$68B–$95B in 2026** (with the dedicated professional VMS and enterprise hardware tier capturing $24B–$30B). 

The industry demonstrates a **moderately concentrated top tier** dominated by global hardware and enterprise VMS manufacturers (where the top leaders account for roughly 30%–43% of total revenue), yet it is structurally underpinned by a **highly fragmented ecosystem** of regional systems integrators, niche physical security providers, and specialized cloud-native/edge-AI startups rather than a winner-take-all monopoly.

## 🛠️ Recommended Architecture Blueprints

Combine these best-in-class components to construct resilient, privacy-respecting video systems:

```
┌─────────────────────────┐       RTSP       ┌─────────────────────────┐
│   Outdoor Weather Cam   │ ───────────────> │  MediaMTX / go2rtc      │
│   or All-Sky Camera     │                  │  (Low-Latency Gateway)  │
└─────────────────────────┘                  └────────────┬────────────┘
                                                          │
                    ┌─────────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────┐
                    ▼                                                                           ▼
     ┌─────────────────────────────┐                                             ┌─────────────────────────────┐
     │  Frigate NVR / Viseron      │                                             │  Allsky / indi-allsky       │
     │  (AI Object Detection / GPU)│                                             │  (Timelapse, Keograms, Sky) │
     └──────────────┬──────────────┘                                             └──────────────┬──────────────┘
                    ▼                                                                           ▼
     ┌─────────────────────────────┐                                             ┌─────────────────────────────┐
     │  Home Assistant Dashboard   │ <────────────────────────────────────────── │  WeeWX / MQTT Station Data  │
     │  & Push Video Alerts        │                                             │  (Temp, Barometer, Rain)    │
     └─────────────────────────────┘                                             └─────────────────────────────┘
```

- **For Weather & All-Sky Timelapse Stations**: Pair **`Allsky`** or **`indi-allsky`** on a Raspberry Pi with a fish-eye lens camera to automatically generate daily keograms, startrail plots, and MP4 daytime sky loops.
- **For Edge AI Perimeter Surveillance**: Ingest H.264/H.265 RTSP streams through **`go2rtc`**, process local YOLO inference via **`Frigate NVR`** with a Google Coral TPU or NVIDIA GPU, and send rich snapshot notifications to Telegram or Home Assistant.
- **For Public Weather Stream Broadcasting**: Use **`MediaMTX`** to convert RTSP camera streams into WebRTC and LL-HLS feeds embedded directly into public weather community websites.

## ⚖️ Disclaimer

- 📌 This is an independent, **community-curated directory** provided for educational and architectural reference—not an official commercial endorsement.
- 🔐 **Privacy & Legal Compliance**: Always ensure your outdoor weather cameras and surveillance installations comply with regional privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR, state wiretap and surveillance statutes). Never capture unconsented private spaces, and place required CCTV warning signs where appropriate.
- 🛡️ **Network Security Best Practices**: Never expose unencrypted camera RTSP ports (`554`) or self-hosted NVR management interfaces directly to the public internet. Always use HTTPS/TLS, WireGuard/Tailscale VPN tunnels, or Cloudflare Zero Trust tunnels.
